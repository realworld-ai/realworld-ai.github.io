We conduct research on Human Activity Recognition (HAR) using sensor data obtained from smartphones, smartwatches, and dedicated wearable devices to recognize everything from basic movements like "walking" and "running" to complex activities such as "cooking" and "packing work." We explore high-accuracy recognition using deep learning and learning methods to reduce labeling costs.
