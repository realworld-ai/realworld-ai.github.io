We research real-world data mining technologies to perform anomaly detection, causal relationship estimation, and future prediction from massive amounts of accumulated real-world sensor data (time-series data). Various real-world applications are anticipated, such as analyzing the behavior of healthy and disease-model animals to aid in drug discovery, evaluating the impact of environmental changes on wild animal behavior, assessing the effects of wildlife damage control measures on animal behavior, and extracting know-how and tacit knowledge from the operational data of skilled workers.
