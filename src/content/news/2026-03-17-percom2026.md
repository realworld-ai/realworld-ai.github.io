<div class="lang-ja">

イタリアのピサで開催されたPerCom2026の本会議にて2本のフルペーパー論文の口頭発表を行いました。PerCom2026は、IoT分野のトップ国際会議の一つです。昨年に引き続き、本研究グループが世界中の研究グループの中で論文が最も多く採択されていました。

![PerCom2026 Presentation](/images/news/Percom2026-1.png)

![PerCom2026 Presentation](/images/news/Percom2026-2.png)

![PerCom2026 Presentation](/images/news/Percom2026-3.png)

*   **Kei Tanigaki, Takuya Maekawa, Takahiro Hara**: HARBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models in Sensor-based Human Activity Recognition
*   **Hongyin Qiao, Qingxin Xia, Hamada Rizk, Takuya Maekawa**: A Teaching Assistant for Teacher-Student Learning: Knowledge Transfer from Skeleton to Inertial Sensing for Activity Recognition in Industrial Domains

</div>

<div class="lang-en">

Two research papers have been presented at the main conference of PerCom2026, which will be held in Pisa, Italy. PerCom2026 is one of the top international conferences in the IoT field. Following last year, our research group had the highest number of accepted papers among research groups worldwide.

*   **Hongyin Qiao, Qingxin Xia, Hamada Rizk, Takuya Maekawa**: A Teaching Assistant for Teacher-Student Learning: Knowledge Transfer from Skeleton to Inertial Sensing for Activity Recognition in Industrial Domains
