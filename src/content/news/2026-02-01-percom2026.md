<div class="lang-ja">

イタリアのピサで開催されるPerCom2026の本会議に2本のフルペーパー論文が採択されました。本会議論文の採択率は約15%でした。PerCom2026は、IoT分野のトップ国際会議の一つです。昨年に引き続き、本研究グループが世界中の研究グループの中で論文が最も多く採択されていました。

*   **Kei Tanigaki, Takuya Maekawa, Takahiro Hara** HARBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models in Sensor-based Human Activity Recognition
*   **Hongyin Qiao, Qingxin Xia, Hamada Rizk, Takuya Maekawa** A Teaching Assistant for Teacher-Student Learning: Knowledge Transfer from Skeleton to Inertial Sensing for Activity Recognition in Industrial Domains

</div>

<div class="lang-en">

Two full papers have been accepted for the main conference of PerCom2026, which will be held in Pisa, Italy. PerCom2026 is one of the top international conferences in the IoT field. Following last year, our research group had the highest number of accepted papers among research groups worldwide.

*   **Kei Tanigaki, Takuya Maekawa, Takahiro Hara** HARBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models in Sensor-based Human Activity Recognition
*   **Hongyin Qiao, Qingxin Xia, Hamada Rizk, Takuya Maekawa** A Teaching Assistant for Teacher-Student Learning: Knowledge Transfer from Skeleton to Inertial Sensing for Activity Recognition in Industrial Domains

</div>
