2025年9月10-12日に韓国の釜山で開催されたICMU2025に参加し、以下のキーノート講演と研究発表を行いました。また、1名の学生がBest Paper Awardを受賞しました。

![ICMU](/images/news/icmu2025_2.png)

*   **Takuya Maekawa** Transforming Animal Behavioral Research with AI and IoT Technologies, Keynote Talk, ICMU 2025, Sept. 2025.
*   **Guanyu Cao, Yasue Kishino, Kazuya Ohara, Takuya Maekawa** Toward Cross-Modal Generalization: Learning Without Direct Supervision for Wi-Fi CSI-Based Depth Imaging, Proc. of the 15th International Conference on Mobile Computing and Ubiquitous Networking (ICMU 2025), pp. 1-8 (Sept. 2025).
*   **Guanyu Cao, Yasue Kishino, Kazuya Ohara, Takuya Maekawa** Best Paper Award, ICMU 2025, Toward Cross-Modal Generalization: Learning Without Direct Supervision for Wi-Fi CSI-Based Depth Imaging, Sept. 2025.
</div>
