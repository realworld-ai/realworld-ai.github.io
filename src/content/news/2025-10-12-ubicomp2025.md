*   **Yang Wang, Heng Zhou, Takuya Maekawa**: Acoustic-assisted Indoor Pedestrian Dead Reckoning, Proc. of the 2025 ACM International Symposium on Wearable Computers (ISWC 2025), pp. 2-8 (Oct. 2025).
*   **Hongyin Qiao, Hamada Rizk, and Takuya Maekawa**: Preliminary Investigation of Knowledge Transfer from Skeleton to IMU for Robust Activity Recognition in Industrial Settings, Companion of the 2025 on ACM International Joint Conference on Pervasive and Ubiquitous Computing , pp. 221-225 (Oct. 2025).
*   **Haruki Yonekura, Kei Tanigaki, Tsuneari Kuroiwa**: What Does AI See through Animals' Eyes?: Zero-Shot Activity Recognition from Bio-loggers, In Companion of the 2025 on ACM International Joint Conference on Pervasive and Ubiquitous Computing(UbiComp), Student Challenge, pp. 617-620 (Oct. 2025).
