Cerebral aneurysms are high-risk conditions with a mortality rate exceeding approximately 50% upon rupture. While direct visualization through highly invasive open-skull surgery has traditionally been used, minimally invasive imaging techniques such as CT and MRI have recently been established.
In this study, we develop a technology to estimate aneurysm wall characteristics by leveraging medical imaging data acquired through these modalities and applying integrated mathematical modeling and AI-driven analysis. Furthermore, we validate the effectiveness of the proposed approach across multiple clinical cases and aim to automate the protocol for real-world clinical deployment.
