Cerebral aneurysms are high-risk diseases with a fatality rate exceeding approximately 50% upon rupture. Instead of highly invasive craniotomy to "visually inspect directly," minimally invasive observation methods using "CT tomography/magnetic resonance imaging" have been established in recent years. In this study, we develop technology to estimate "aneurysm wall properties" by utilizing medical data captured by these methods and leveraging the fusion analysis of mathematics and AI. Furthermore, we verify its effectiveness across multiple cases and aim for protocol automation toward social implementation.
