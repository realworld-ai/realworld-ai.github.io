This study elucidates super-sensory integration mechanisms beyond the five senses by learning from diverse marine organisms that have acquired super-sensory integration through evolution, utilizing measurement technologies inside and outside the organism and movement data analysis methods. To this end, we develop interdisciplinary research organically linking neuroscience, ecology, and data science across long-distance navigation species including birds and fish. Furthermore, we comprehensively understand super-sensory integration mechanisms by performing intervention operations inside and outside the organism using experimental animals such as mice.
