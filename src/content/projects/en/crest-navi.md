This project investigates super-sensory integration mechanisms acquired through evolution by leveraging in vivo and ex vivo measurement technologies and movement data analysis methods. By studying diverse long-distance navigating species—including birds and fish—we aim to uncover sensory integration mechanisms that extend beyond the conventional five senses.
The research integrates neuroscience, ecology, and data science in a cross-species framework. In addition, interventional experiments using laboratory animals such as mice are conducted to achieve a comprehensive understanding of super-sensory integration mechanisms.
