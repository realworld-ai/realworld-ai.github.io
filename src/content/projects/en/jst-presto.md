This project aims to develop a work support framework that enables humans and AI to learn and evolve together by integrating wearable sensor data with language-based task knowledge. We develop methods for workers and AI to collaboratively identify workplace challenges, propose and validate improvements, and support skill development and continuous improvement in manufacturing and logistics environments.
